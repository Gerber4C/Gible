# Suggested Signoff Semantics

## Problem

Gible currently treats many calls tagged `signoff` as irrevocable terminal
placements.  In particular, its purpose axiom creates an automatic Pass when
the preceding contract also has `final_placement` and
`purpose_completed=True`.  This is wrong for a bridge *signoff suggestion*:
the caller has declined to cooperate, but an exceptionally strong partner may
still continue.

Two Daqiush 1NT routes demonstrate the bug:

1. `1NT–2C–2M–3oM–3NT–4x–4M`, where 4M follows a shown shortage; and
2. `1NT–2C–2M–4D–4M`, where 4M declines the no-shortage slam try.

The second route already authors both Pass and 4NT on the next turn, but the
terminal-purpose shortcut pre-empts both candidates.

## Terminology and contract

`signoff` is the English label for a bid that recommends stopping at the
current contract.  It is not, by itself, an irrevocable end to the auction.

There are two distinct kinds of placement:

| Kind | Required metadata | Effect on partner's next call |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Suggested signoff | `signoff`; may also be a natural placement | Pass remains available, but authored or derived continuations remain eligible. |
| Final placement | `final_placement` and `purpose_completed=True` | The purpose axiom may synthesize the terminal Pass and suppress further choices. |

`final_placement` may still be used to describe a contract level for display
or scoring, but it must never be sufficient on its own to close a purpose.
Only `purpose_completed=True` authorizes the terminal-purpose shortcut.

## Scope

This change is deliberately limited to the two verified Daqiush families and
the generic terminal-pass boundary that causes them to fail.  It does not
reclassify all existing game placements, serious-3NT endings, Texas
completions, or keycard placements.  Those need separate bidding-rule review.

### Shortage route

For every major/shortage combination generated by
`stayman_*_after_*_shortage_stop`:

- 4M (or Pass where the displayed shortage call is already 4M) is a suggested
  signoff, not a completed purpose;
- the responder may Pass;
- with an agreed major, an eligible normal RKCB continuation remains available
  through the existing derived/authoring mechanism;
- an Exclusion/Voidwood candidate must not supersede that normal continuation
  merely because the short suit has already been publicly shown.

### No-shortage route

For `stayman_*_no_shortage_decline`:

- 4M is a suggested signoff;
- the explicitly authored responder choices after it remain visible: Pass for
  a genuine stop and 4NT for RKCB;
- the existing predicate deciding whether a responder has enough to ask
  keycards remains authoritative.

## Engine behavior

1. `terminal_pass_candidate` continues to require both `final_placement` and
   `purpose_completed=True`.
2. Call specs that mean only suggested signoff omit `purpose_completed`; they
   retain the `signoff` act so display, axioms, and policy vocabulary remain
   consistent.
3. Candidate generation/selection must evaluate normal authored candidates
   before applying derived terminal Pass.  A generic derived Pass is a
   fallback, never a substitute for an explicit continuation.
4. For the shortage route, the selected continuation must be based on the
   real hand and established trump.  The shown shortage is public information,
   not a reason to reinterpret a subsequent normal 4NT as Voidwood.

## Non-goals

- Replacing all authored continuations with a universal slam policy.
- Changing the meaning of a true final placement, accepted/rejected grand-slam
  invitation, or a completed keycard sequence.
- Implementing the broader extreme-hand descriptive proxy in this change; that
  is specified separately in
  `2026-10-30-extreme-hand-description-proxy-design.md`.

## Tests

Focused tests must cover:

1. the screenshot auction and hands: North selects 4H as a suggested signoff;
   South is not automatically passed and has the intended continuation set;
2. the symmetric spade and other-shortage variants;
3. `1NT–2C–2M–4D–4M`: a weak responder passes and a strong responder reaches
   the authored 4NT path;
4. genuine completed placements still synthesize a terminal Pass;
5. no unrelated 1NT invitation or RKCB test is broadened unnecessarily.

Run the smallest affected test selection first: the owner-route tests,
one-notrump invitation tests, and the terminal-purpose/derived-semantics tests.
