## Requirements retained from discussion

- RHO non-Pass permits a forcing/invitational Pass; RHO Pass does not.
- Invite when guaranteed total is 1-2 points short and partner maximum can supply the gap.
- Acceptance: width 1 maximum; width 2 good middle or maximum; width >=3 lower+2.
- GF strict game by default, optionally explicit 4C/4D exceptions tied to an origin or a concrete context.
- No fabricated Pass or call when obligations have no compliant candidate.
- Strength is own HCP plus public partner evidence, never actual partner cards.
- Quality uses Pavlicek's published Kaplan–Rubens algorithm; the project classification is K–R minus HCP >= 0.5, configurable and explicitly distinguished from source rules.
