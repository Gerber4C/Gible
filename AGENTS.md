# Gible Agent Instructions

## Owner terminology

### “逼局” / game forcing

When the owner says a call or auction is **逼局** (game forcing), it means:

- the partnership is forced to at least game;
- **slam-oriented hands are not excluded**;
- it is not a synonym for “game only”, “signoff at game”, or “no slam interest”.

Do not default to game-only placement or suppress slam routes merely because
the owner used the word “逼局”. A game-forcing auction may continue with cues,
keycard asks, and slam placement unless the owner explicitly says “止叫”,
“只打局”, or otherwise limits the hand to game.
